The Best Time to Buy: Seasonal Shopping Calendar for 2026

Published May 29, 2026 · 5 min read

Timing is everything when it comes to saving money. The difference between paying full price and getting a great deal is often just a matter of waiting a few weeks. Here's our month-by-month guide to the best shopping windows in 2026.

The Golden Rule of Seasonal Shopping

Every product category has a predictable price cycle. Buy after the peak season, not before. Winter coats are cheapest in February, not October. Grills are cheapest in August, not May. Smart shoppers buy out of season and save 30–60%.

January

Fitness gear, bedding, and winter apparel clearance. New Year sales are real — retailers clear holiday overstock.

Best for: Gym equipment, sheets, towels

February

Super Bowl TV deals, winter clearance continues. Presidents' Day sales on mattresses and appliances.

Best for: TVs, mattresses, winter coats

March

Spring cleaning sales on home appliances. Last season's electronics models start discounting for new releases.

Best for: Vacuum cleaners, cookware, laptops

April

Tax season promotions. Amazon Spring Sale. Garden and outdoor gear starts going on sale.

Best for: Tools, gardening equipment, tax refund deals

May

Memorial Day sales are one of the biggest sale events. Major discounts on mattresses, furniture, and appliances.

Best for: Furniture, mattresses, power tools

June

Amazon Prime Day (usually July, but early deals start in June). End of financial year for some retailers.

Best for: Electronics, headphones, smart home

July

Amazon Prime Day is the biggest online shopping event of the summer. Target Deal Days and Walmart Deals for Days follow.

Best for: Amazon devices, tablets, SD cards

August

Back-to-school sales on laptops, dorm essentials. Summer clearance on grills, patio furniture.

Best for: Laptops, backpacks, outdoor furniture

September

Labor Day sales. New iPhone release triggers discounts on previous models. Appliances go on sale.

Best for: Last-gen phones, washers/dryers

October

Target Circle Week. Early holiday deals begin. Columbus Day sales on clothing and home goods.

Best for: Jeans, sweaters, Halloween items

November

Black Friday and Cyber Monday. The biggest shopping weekend of the year. TVs, laptops, and toys hit their lowest prices.

Best for: TVs, gaming consoles, headphones

December

Pre-Christmas last-minute deals. After Christmas: the biggest clearance sales of the year. Everything must go.

Best for: Decorations, toys, holiday items (after 12/26)

What NOT to Buy Full Price

  • Electronics — Never buy at launch unless you absolutely need it. Prices drop 15–25% within 3 months.
  • Furniture — Memorial Day, Labor Day, and Presidents' Day are the big three. Buy then or wait.
  • Clothing — End-of-season clearance saves you 50–70%. Buy winter clothes in February, summer clothes in August.
  • Toys — Black Friday and post-Christmas are the cheapest times of the year.
  • Fitness equipment — January (New Year) is surprisingly good, but February–March clearance is even better.
