4th of July Sales 2026: Best Deals on Grills, Electronics & Outdoor Gear

Published June 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Prime Day just ended, but don't put your wallet away just yet. The 4th of July sales are here, and they're bigger than ever in 2026. With inflation still sitting at 4.2% and gas hovering above $4 a gallon, every dollar counts — and the retailers know it.

Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy, Walmart, and Amazon are all rolling out 4th of July specials this week. The good news? You can save serious money on grills, patio furniture, electronics, and outdoor gear. The bad news? Not every "sale" is a real deal, and the same item can cost $50–100 more at one store versus another.

Best Categories to Shop This 4th of July

1. Grills & Smokers — Peak Season Pricing

July 4th is the absolute best time to buy a grill. Retailers are competing hard because everyone is hosting cookouts. Last year's models are at clearance prices, and this year's models are getting 20–30% off.

What to look for: Weber Spirit II E-310 under $450, Traeger Pro 575 under $700, Blackstone 36-inch griddle under $280. Check Home Depot vs Lowe's vs Amazon — we've seen $60–80 differences on the same grill.

2. Patio Furniture — End-of-Season Sneak Peak

Technically patio season isn't over, but retailers start clearing space for fall inventory right after July 4th. That means 30–50% off patio sets, fire pits, and outdoor decor. The selection is still good — by August, it's picked over.

3. TVs & Electronics — Post-Prime Day Continuation

A lot of the Prime Day TV deals are still active, and Best Buy and Walmart are price-matching. 65-inch 4K TVs from Samsung and LG are available under $600. If you missed Prime Day, this is your second chance — and you don't need a Prime membership to get the price.

4. Outdoor Power Tools — Mowers, Trimmers & Leaf Blowers

Home Depot and Lowe's 4th of July sales are the best time to buy lawn equipment. DeWalt, Milwaukee, and EGO tools are 20–40% off. Battery-powered mowers are especially competitive this year as gas prices push more people to electric.

5. Camping & Outdoor Gear — Summer Adventures

If you're heading out for a 4th of July camping trip, REI, Walmart, and Amazon are all running outdoor gear sales. Tents, coolers, camping stoves, and sleeping bags are 20–50% off. The Yeti Roadie 24 cooler has been spotted at $150 (normally $200) — the lowest price of the year so far.

6. Back-to-School Early Bird Deals

It's only June, but back-to-school sales have started early. Laptops, backpacks, and dorm essentials are 15–30% off right now. The prices will only go up as August gets closer. A decent Chromebook for $149 and a HP laptop for $299 are solid deals.

What to Skip This 4th of July

  • Indoor furniture — Labor Day (early September) has much better deals on sofas, beds, and dining sets.
  • Major appliances — Labor Day and Black Friday are better for fridges, washers, and dryers.
  • Summer clothing — End-of-August clearance is where the real clothing deals are, not July 4th.
  • Mattresses — Presidents' Day and Labor Day are the mattress sales events.

Store-by-Store Breakdown

  • Home Depot — Best for grills, power tools, and patio furniture. Their 4th of July sale is one of the biggest of the year.
  • Lowe's — Price-matches Home Depot and often has better clearance on last-season grills. Worth comparing.
  • Best Buy — The place for post-Prime Day TV and laptop deals. Price-match guarantee makes it easy.
  • Walmart — Household essentials, toys, and camping gear. No membership needed.
  • Amazon — Many Prime Day deals are still active. Check if the price dropped again.
  • eBay — Open-box and refurbished grills and electronics are 30–50% off retail. Worth checking.

4 Tips for Scoring the Best 4th of July Deals

  • Shop early. The best deals sell out by midday July 3rd. Don't wait until the 4th.
  • Check for price matching. Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy, and Target all offer price matching. If you find a lower price at a competitor, they'll match it.
  • Look for clearance on last year's models. Grills and lawn equipment from 2025 are being cleared out at 40–60% off. The 2026 model is rarely worth the premium.
  • Use SnappRice. The fastest way to compare prices across all stores. One search, all the prices, click straight to the best deal.

Bottom Line

The 4th of July sales are legit — but only if you shop smart. Markups are real, urgency is manufactured, and the same product can vary by $100 depending on where you buy. Take five minutes to compare before you click "buy." Your wallet will thank you.
