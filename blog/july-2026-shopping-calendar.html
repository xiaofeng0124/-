July 2026 Shopping Calendar: The Best Sales and Deals Happening This Month

Published June 28, 2026 · 5 min read

Prime Day is over. The 4th of July sales are here. And August is closer than you think. July 2026 is shaping up to be a huge month for deals — but only if you know when and where to shop.

Between Independence Day clearance, summer closeouts, and early back-to-school sales, there's a lot happening. Here's your week-by-week guide to the best deals in July 2026.

💡 Bookmark this page. Before you buy anything in July, check the price on SnappRice. We compare prices across Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot, Lowe's, and eBay — so you never overpay.

Week 1 (June 29 — July 5): 4th of July Fireworks

This is the biggest shopping week of the month. Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy, Walmart, and Amazon are all running 4th of July sales. Here's what to buy:

  • Grills & Smokers — Peak discounts. Weber, Traeger, and Blackstone are 20-40% off. Compare Home Depot vs Lowe's — prices vary significantly.
  • Patio Furniture — 30-50% off as retailers clear space for fall. Best selection of the year right now.
  • Outdoor Power Tools — Lawn mowers, trimmers, and leaf blowers at 20-40% off. DeWalt and EGO battery kits are particularly good deals.
  • TVs — Post-Prime Day prices are still active. 65-inch 4K TVs under $600 at Best Buy and Walmart.
  • Camping Gear — Tents, coolers, and sleeping bags at 20-50% off. REI's 4th of July sale ends July 5.

Week 2 (July 6-12): The Calm Before the Storm

This is the quietest week of the month. Most major sales have ended. But it's a great time to buy items that didn't sell during 4th of July:

  • Leftover 4th of July Clearance — Grills, patio furniture, and outdoor decor are being cleared at additional 10-20% off.
  • Summer Apparel — First wave of summer clothing clearance starts now. 40-60% off at Target and Walmart.
  • Amazon Prime Day leftovers — Some deals are still active. Check SnappRice for lingering discounts.

Week 3 (July 13-19): Amazon Prime Day 2.0 & Early Back-to-School

Amazon often runs a "Prime Day 2.0" or "Prime Day Flash Sale" about 2-3 weeks after the main event. Also, back-to-school sales start in earnest:

  • Laptops & Tablets — Chromebooks under $200, iPads under $300, Windows laptops under $400. Target and Best Buy are aggressive on pricing.
  • Backpacks & Lunch Boxes — 30-50% off at Walmart and Target. The earlier you buy, the better the selection.
  • Dorm Essentials — Bedding, storage, and mini-fridges go on sale mid-July. Don't wait until August.

Week 4 (July 20-26): Summer Closeout & Prime Day Echoes

Retailers start looking toward fall. Summer items hit deep clearance, and back-to-school picks up steam:

  • Summer Clearance — 50-70% off outdoor furniture, grills (display models), and summer decor.
  • Swimwear & Summer Clothing — 60-75% off at most retailers. Stock up for next year.
  • Electronics — Back-to-school laptop deals continue. Good time to buy noise-canceling headphones for dorm life.

Week 5 (July 27-31): Pre-August Prep

The last week of July is all about back-to-school. August prices will be higher and stock will be thinner:

  • School Supplies — Notebooks, pens, binders at buy-one-get-one or 50% off at Staples and Office Depot.
  • Dorm Furniture — Bed risers, fans, and storage solutions go on sale. Target and Walmart are the go-to.
  • Last Call Summer — Final wave of summer clearance. Patio furniture hits 70% off, but selection is limited.
