Back to School Shopping Guide 2026: Best Deals on Laptops, Supplies & Dorm Essentials

Published July 15, 2026 · 7 min read

It's mid-July, which means back-to-school season is officially here. Whether you're heading to college, sending kids to elementary school, or stocking up for remote learning, this is the time to shop — and the savings are massive if you know where to look.

Retailers across the board are rolling out their biggest deals of the summer. Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and Staples are all competing for your back-to-school dollars, and that competition means one thing: serious discounts. The trick is knowing which store offers the best price on what you actually need.

Laptops & Tablets: Where to Get the Best Deals

This is the biggest expense on most back-to-school lists. A good laptop can cost anywhere from $300 to $2,000+, so finding the right price matters. Here's what we're seeing in July 2026:

Budget Laptops ($250–$500)

Chromebooks and entry-level Windows laptops dominate this range. Walmart and Amazon consistently have the best prices here, often $20–$50 below MSRP. Look for Chromebooks with at least 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage for the best value. Acer and Lenovo models in this range are solid choices for basic schoolwork, browsing, and streaming.

Mid-Range Laptops ($500–$900)

This is the sweet spot for most college students. Dell XPS, HP Envy, and Lenovo ThinkPad models frequently hit this price range during back-to-school sales. Best Buy often has the best selection here, but Amazon typically edges them out on price by 5–10%. Use a price comparison tool to check both before pulling the trigger.

Premium Laptops ($900+)

MacBook Air M4, MacBook Pro, Dell XPS 16, and Surface Laptop 7 — these are premium machines for students who need serious performance. The MacBook Air M4 has been as low as $949 during recent sales. Amazon and Best Buy are the most competitive here, with Target occasionally offering gift card bonuses that effectively lower the price.

Tablets for School

iPad (10th gen) and Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE are the top picks for students who want a tablet for note-taking and reading. The iPad is typically $349 at most retailers, but during back-to-school sales, it can drop to $299. Amazon tends to have the lowest iPad prices, while Samsung tablets are often cheapest at Best Buy or direct from Samsung with a student discount.

🔍 Real deal check (July 2026): The MacBook Air M4 is currently $949 at Amazon ($150 off), $979 at Best Buy, and $999 at Target with a $50 gift card. That makes Amazon the clear winner — but check current prices at SnappRice before you buy.

Backpacks & Bags: Target and Amazon Lead the Way

Backpacks are a category where prices vary wildly. High-end brands like JanSport, North Face, and Herschel can cost $60–$120, while store brands offer similar quality for $15–$30.

Target has the best selection of affordable backpacks, with their in-house brand starting at just $12.99. The designs are surprisingly good for the price. Amazon has the widest variety, with prices from $15 to $150. Walmart is the cheapest for basic backpacks, with some models under $10.

For college students who need laptop-compatible backpacks, Best Buy carries SwissGear and Targus models that are specifically designed for tech — and they often bundle them with laptop accessories.

Dorm Room Essentials: Big Savings on Big Purchases

If you're heading to college, dorm shopping can easily cost $500–$1,500. Here's where to save on the big-ticket items:

Bedding & Linens

Twin XL sheets, comforters, and pillows are best bought at Target or Walmart. Target's Threshold brand offers good quality at reasonable prices, while Walmart's Mainstays brand is the budget champion. Amazon has some great mattress toppers — crucial for dorm beds — starting around $30.

Mini Fridges & Microwaves

Best Buy and Amazon are the go-to for dorm appliances. A 3.2 cu ft mini fridge runs $120–$180 depending on features. Best Buy often has open-box options for 20% off. Microwave ovens start at $40 for basic models.

Desk Lamps & Lighting

Amazon has the widest selection of LED desk lamps with USB charging ports (perfect for dorms with limited outlets). Prices start at $15 for basic models. IKEA is also an option, but keep in mind shipping costs if you're not near a store.

Storage & Organization

Target and Walmart have excellent storage solutions — bins, under-bed organizers, and closet systems. Target's Room Essentials line is affordable and looks good. Amazon has cube storage organizers that are popular with dorm residents.

📌 Money-saving tip: Check if your college has a "dorm buy/sell" Facebook group. Upperclassmen moving out often sell mini fridges, microwaves, and furniture for 50–70% less than retail. Just make sure you can pick it up before move-in day.

Clothing & Uniforms: Shop Smart, Not Hard

Back-to-school clothing is a major expense for K-12 families. Target offers the best balance of price and style for kids' clothing with their Cat & Jack brand, which starts at $5 for basic tees and includes a one-year satisfaction guarantee. Walmart is cheaper (t-shirts from $3.98) but the selection is narrower. Amazon has the widest range, and Prime members get free returns — helpful when sizes don't work out.

For school uniforms, Amazon and Walmart have the best prices on basic polo shirts and khaki pants. Target's uniform selection has improved significantly and offers competitive pricing with better fabric quality.

Tech Accessories Every Student Needs

Beyond the laptop itself, students need a few key accessories. Here's where to find the best deals:

  • Wireless headphones / earbuds — Amazon and Best Buy battle for the best prices. AirPods Pro 2 have been as low as $179, while solid alternatives from Anker and JBL start at $25.
  • External hard drives / SSDs — Amazon is usually cheapest. A 1TB portable SSD runs $60–$90. Essential for backing up term papers.
  • Printers — Best Buy has the best selection. A basic HP or Canon inkjet is $40–$70. Just be ready for the ink costs — consider an HP Instant Ink subscription to save.
  • USB-C hubs & adapters — Amazon dominates this category. A 7-in-1 USB-C hub costs $15–$30.
  • Laptop sleeves and cases — Target and Amazon have good options from $10–$30.

Use Student Discounts for Extra Savings

Many retailers and services offer student discounts that stack on top of sale prices. If you have a .edu email address, you can save on:

  • Apple Education Pricing — Save $100–$150 on MacBooks and iPads, plus get free AirPods during promo periods
  • Best Buy Student Deals — Exclusive discounts on laptops and tablets
  • Amazon Prime Student — Half-price Prime membership ($7.49/month) with access to Prime Day deals
  • Spotify / Apple Music Student — Discounted plans that include Hulu or Apple TV+
  • Microsoft 365 Education — Free with a .edu email address
