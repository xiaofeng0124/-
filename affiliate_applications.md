# 联盟计划申请文案

## 通用信息

- **Website URL**: https://snapprice.co
- **Website Name**: SnappRice
- **Website Description**: A price comparison engine that helps shoppers find the best deals across major US retailers.
- **Content Type**: Comparison shopping, product reviews, deal aggregation
- **Target Audience**: US-based online shoppers, bargain hunters, tech enthusiasts
- **Social Media**: N/A (currently focused on organic search traffic)

---

## 1. eBay Partner Network

**申请地址**: https://partner.ebay.com/

### Site Description (网站描述):
> SnappRice is a next-generation price comparison platform that enables shoppers to search and compare prices across multiple major US retailers in real-time. Users can search by product name, voice command, or image upload. We feature price history charts, price drop alerts, and personalized deal recommendations. Our mission is to help consumers make informed purchasing decisions by showing them every available option at a glance.

### Promotion Methods (推广方式):
> We will integrate eBay product listings directly into our search results alongside other retailers. When a user searches for a product, SnappRice will display eBay listings with price, condition, and seller rating information. Users can then click through to complete their purchase on eBay. We will also feature eBay deals and auctions in our "Popular Products" section and price alert system.

---

## 2. Best Buy Creator Program

**申请地址**: https://creators.bestbuy.com

### Application Description:
> SnappRice is a US-focused price comparison engine that helps shoppers find the best deals on electronics and tech products. Users search for products and see real-time price comparisons across major retailers including Amazon, Walmart, and others. We want to integrate Best Buy as a key pricing source — especially for electronics, computers, gaming, and home theater products where Best Buy is a trusted leader.

### How will you promote Best Buy?:
> We will display Best Buy pricing and product availability in our search results whenever relevant. For electronics and tech searches — which make up a large portion of our product catalog — Best Buy will be featured prominently as a trusted retailer. Our price history charts, price drop alerts, and product comparison views will all include Best Buy listings. We also plan to feature Best Buy exclusive deals and clearance items in our deals section.

### Target Audience:
> US-based online shoppers actively searching for electronics, computers, gaming consoles, headphones, smart home devices, and appliances.

---

## 3. Target Affiliates

**申请地址**: https://partners.target.com

### Website Description:
> SnappRice (snapprice.co) is a comprehensive price comparison engine serving the US market. Users can search for any product — from groceries and household essentials to electronics and apparel — and see pricing from multiple major retailers side by side. We provide price history tracking, price drop alerts, and personalized deal discovery. Our platform is designed to help US consumers save time and money by eliminating the need to check multiple stores individually.

### Promotion Strategy:
> Target products will be integrated into our search results across relevant categories including home & kitchen, apparel, beauty, electronics, toys, and grocery. We will highlight Target's competitive pricing and weekly deals. Target links will appear with clear product images, pricing, and availability information. Our price alert system will also allow users to set target prices and be notified when prices drop at Target.

### Audience & Content:
> Our users are US-based shoppers actively comparing prices before making purchase decisions. We attract users searching for a wide range of products — from everyday essentials to big-ticket items. SnappRice generates comparison shopping intent traffic, meaning users who see Target listings are already in a buying mindset.
