1. Information We Collect

When you use SnappRice, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address and password (hashed) when you register

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our price comparison service

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • SerpAPI — for product search and price data
  • Resend — for transactional emails (verification, password reset)
  • Cloudflare — for hosting and CDN
  • Google OAuth — optional sign-in (if you choose to use it)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

SnappRice participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. We may earn commissions from qualifying purchases made through links on our site. Prices and availability are subject to change.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. No tracking cookies are used without your consent.

6. Data Security

Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Session tokens expire after 7 days.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, use our contact form or email us at privacy@snapprice.co.
