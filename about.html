About SnappRice

One search, every store. We help you find the best price — plain and simple.

What We Do

SnappRice is a free price comparison engine that searches across Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Best Buy, Target, and more. Instead of checking each store one by one, you search once and see all the prices side by side. Whether you're shopping for electronics, home goods, fashion, or everyday essentials — we help you make the call.

Why We Built It

Online shopping should be straightforward. But with so many retailers, it's easy to overpay without knowing it. We built SnappRice to take the hassle out of price hunting — so you can shop with confidence, knowing you've seen what's out there.

How It Works

  • Search — type a product name, speak it, or upload a photo
  • Compare — we pull prices from major retailers and show them in one clean view
  • Save — click through to the best deal, set price alerts, or track price history

What Makes Us Different

  • Unbiased results — we surface prices from multiple retailers, not just the ones that pay us
  • Price history — see how a price has moved over time so you know if it's actually a good deal
  • Price alerts — set a target price and we'll notify you when it drops
  • Photo search — see something in the wild? Snap a picture and find it online

Transparency

SnappRice is an independent comparison service. We participate in affiliate programs (including Amazon Associates), which means we may earn a small commission if you make a purchase through a link on our site. This comes at no extra cost to you — and it never influences which stores or products appear in your results.

Behind the Name

"SnappRice" comes from the idea of snapping up a good deal — quick, smart, and satisfying. Rice? Well, everyone loves a good grain of savings.
